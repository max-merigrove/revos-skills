# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the plan, the letters and the chat summary before delivery. All eight must pass. If one fails, fix the inputs and rebuild; never caveat a weak output.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every figure from the script | Every fee, date, cash figure, break-even, scenario and concession cost in the chat and the files appears in the script's output. Cash counts Option A for a scope-reset client (it applies if they do not reply), and the plan says what Option B would take off. The workbook holds live formulas where a founder would change an input (fees, hours, costs, cap, leaves) and recalculates to the script's figures. |
| 2 | No blanket rise | Every client has a group, a reason (or `[NEED: one true reason]`) and a new fee or "hold". Held clients are named and get no letter. A client whose last step falls short of target shows the gap and the steps to close it. A plan with no hours says plainly that it is a blanket rise. |
| 3 | Break-even shown two ways | The share of the raised revenue that can leave, and the named departures in both orders, saying which departure puts the plan behind. With ten or fewer raised clients it says the count is lumpy. |
| 4 | Dates from the founder's terms | Each effective date is the first billing date after the founder's notice (aligned to a period end where their notice says so), or the renewal. Each date is marked founder supplied or planning default; a planning default HOLDs the letter. Nothing says a date meets a contract or a law. Nothing is backdated or "effective immediately". |
| 5 | Letters that hold | New fee and date in the first three lines. One true reason traced to something the founder said, true for that client (never the new-client price as the reason for a fee above it), shown beside the client in the Plan sheet. Written to the client as "you", never about them as "they". No apology, no invented cost or market claim, what stays the same, a stated choice, no fake urgency. |
| 6 | Pushback costs something visible | Four replies, each in a hold and a trade version. Every concession is a trade with an end date, logged with its cost. Never a discount for nothing. |
| 7 | Specific and sendable | Names, fees and dates are filled from the list. Anything unknown is `[NEED: ...]` and its letter is HOLD. A stranger could send a READY letter with no edits except the founder's sign-off. The founder's own words, clients and priorities are used throughout. |
| 8 | Clean and global | `letters.py check` and `check_copy.py` return 0 problems. The founder's English, currency, number style and date order throughout. No tax rate or tax amount; fees before any sales tax with the neutral line. The closing Business Audit line appears once, in chat only, with its `[BUSINESS_AUDIT_URL]` placeholder left as it is (it is filled at release, not by the skill). The files carry the founder's business name and nothing of the tool that made them, beyond the file properties. |
