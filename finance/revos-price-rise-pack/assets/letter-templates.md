# Letter templates

The words `letters.py` uses, with merge fields in square brackets. Use them by hand only when code cannot run; fill every field from the plan, and mark a letter HOLD if any field is unknown. The standard is in `references/letters.md`.

## Raise now

Subject: Your fee from [effective date]

Hi [first name], (or "Hello," with no contact name)

From [effective date], your fee will be [new fee] [a month], up from [current fee].
[the one reason]
Everything else stays as it is: [what stays the same].
If you are happy to carry on, there is nothing you need to do: the new fee will be on the invoice dated [effective date]. If you would like to talk it through first, reply to this email and we will find a time before [reply-by date].
Thank you for the work we have done together since [year they started].
[sign-off]

## Two steps

From [step 1 date], your fee will be [step 1 fee] [a month], up from [current fee], and from [step 2 date] it will be [step 2 fee].
I am giving you both dates now so you can plan ahead.
[the one reason], then as Raise now.

## At renewal

Subject: Your fee when we renew on [renewal date]
When your current term ends, your fee from [renewal date] will be [new fee] [a month], up from [current fee].
[the one reason]
Everything else stays as it is: [what stays the same].
Reply to confirm you would like to renew on that basis, or to find a time to talk it through before [reply-by date].

## Reset the scope

Subject: Your fee from [effective date]: two options
From [effective date], your fee moves to one of two options.
Option A: the same work as today for [new fee] [a month], up from [current fee].
Option B: [current fee] [a month] as now, for a defined [included hours] hours a month, with anything beyond that at [your hourly fee] an hour, agreed with you before it starts.
[the one reason: how the work has grown, in your words]
Reply with A or B by [reply-by date]. If I have not heard from you by then, Option A applies from [effective date].

## Price to keep

From [effective date], your fee will be [fee to keep] [a month], up from [current fee].
[the one reason]
That is the fee at which I can keep doing this work for you.
If it does not fit your plans, tell me by [reply-by date] and we will agree how to finish the current work.

## Reminder (about two weeks before the date)

A reminder that from [effective date] your fee is [new fee].

## Silence follow-up (five working days, no reply)

Hi [first name], a quick check that my note of [send date] reached you: from [effective date] your fee is [new fee]. Reply if you would like to talk it through before then.

## Confirmation (a client agreed different terms)

Confirming what we agreed: your fee stays at [current fee] until [end of concession], and from [date] it is [agreed fee] [a month].
Everything else stays as it is. Thank you for working this through with me.
