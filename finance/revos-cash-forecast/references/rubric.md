# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the output before delivery. Every criterion must pass (8 of 8). If one fails, rework the output; do not caveat it.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opens on the real balance and the real list | The opening balance is the last balance in the export (or a ledger balance printed in it, or the founder's stated balance when the export has none), with its date and where it came from, in the reading and on the Assumptions tab; an export more than 10 days old is flagged; every regular bill, pay run and client in the export (or carried from memory) is in the forecast, and no payee seen three or more times is filed as a one-off |
| 2 | Cash lands when it really lands | Every receipt is placed on its due date plus that client's known lateness; listed invoices and regular clients are their own lines; a client with listed invoices is not also in the usual pattern or the regular clients; the usual pattern starts after the payment terms when invoices are listed, names the months it averages and is absent in months the founder called quiet; nothing is counted as cash in the week it was sold |
| 3 | Tax and payroll as the founder listed them | Every tax, payroll tax or government payment in the forecast is one the founder listed (what, amount, date or rhythm), on its own dated line, labelled founder supplied with who gave the figure; none is projected from the export or placed by a country's rules; a missing amount is `[NEED]`, never worked out; the reading names who to confirm the figures with |
| 4 | A real bad quarter | The stress case cuts money in by 20 per cent and moves it 15 days later (or the founder's approved figures), applies the payment plan default, leaves outgoings unchanged, and is stated in plain words in the reading |
| 5 | The line and the week | The safety line is stated with its basis; the first breach week is named by number and start date in each case, or "no breach in 13 weeks"; runway is quoted from the bad quarter only, against the 8-week minimum; the headline names the payment that causes the breach |
| 6 | Three dated moves | Exactly three moves in date order, each with a date on or after today, each tied to a named line in the workbook and a figure from the forecast, each one that fits how this business is paid; the re-run date is on its own line, not one of the three; none holds pay, a payroll tax, or a payment tied to booked work; a tested commitment gets yes, a later start date, or not yet, with the reason |
| 7 | Nothing invented, no regulated advice | Every figure in the reading, the note and the chat appears in the script output; gaps are `[NEED: ...]`; no statistic about other businesses; no tax amount, employer cost, interest, penalty or borrowing advice from the skill; no statement of what a law requires or when a tax is due |
| 8 | Clean, usable and in the founder's terms | The workbook opens with live formulas (or the CSV fallback) and the yellow cells move every week; the one-page reading prints on one page of the founder's paper size (Letter or A4, from their country); `check_copy.py --currency` reports 0 problems; figures are in the founder's currency and written their way (R729 891, 1.200,50 €), dates in their order, with nothing assumed from a country; when the founder asked about a month past the 13 weeks, the reading says so; the note and chat mirror the founder's English; no em or en dashes; the planning-not-advice line is present; the Merigrove line appears once, at the end of the closing message, and nowhere in the files; the chat carries the headline, the bad quarter, the deciding receipt and the three moves with their figures; the founder saw a provisional answer before any question, and the run asked at most five questions in all |
