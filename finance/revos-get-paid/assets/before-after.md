# Before and after: the same founder, plain Claude and Get Paid

Landing page material. One illustrative founder: a solo US pricing consultant. Three clients behind: his biggest asked to split an $8,500 invoice; another always pays late, owes two invoices after a $1,000 part payment, and his contract charges 1.5% a month on late invoices; a third disputes $1,900. He asks: "Can I actually charge that?"

## Plain Claude (no skill)

> Yes, generally you can charge it, as long as it's in the contract... some states cap the interest rate... At 1.5% per month for roughly two months, that's about $190 in interest, so around $6,540 in total.
>
> (three emails: "three monthly payments of about $2,833"; "please arrange payment within 7 days... I may need to pause work on the retainer")

Kind, sensible tone. But the interest is estimated by the month, the plan has no dates and does not add up, the pause threat arrives as a maybe in the first email, nothing says what to send next week, and next month it starts from nothing.

## With Get Paid

> **You are owed $14,850.00 on 3 late invoices from 2 clients. Your contract terms add $186.37 so far on Bluefin, $3.13 a day; Copperline left out, as you asked.**
>
> | Client | Owed | Days late | Next message | When |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Copperline Health | $8,500.00 | 36 | call and payment plan offer | today |
> | Bluefin Logistics | $6,350.00 | 62 | late fee notice and payment plan offer | today |
>
> **Copperline:** "Either pay $8,500.00 by October 27, 2026, or split it into 3 installments: $2,833.33 by October 27, $2,833.33 by November 27, $2,833.34 by December 28."
>
> **Bluefin:** interest worked daily at 18% a year from the day after each due date, less on the $1,000.00 they paid, with the working; the same plan offered; the final notice, naming the pause he chose, dated for October 28, after the pay-by date.

Plus one Word document: who to chase in order, every message for today and later dated, the plans, an escalation ladder that ends in "stop work" or "a collections service or lawyer" as options to take advice on, and draft deposit, milestone and card-on-file terms to check with his lawyer. A memory file means next month is "who paid?" and one re-run.

## The difference in five seconds

| | Plain Claude | Get Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Who to chase first | No | Ranked, with the next message and date |
| Interest from his own clause | "About $190" | $186.37, worked daily, part payment counted, working shown |
| Payment plan | "About $2,833" a month | Three dated installments summing to $8,500.00 |
| What to send if they still don't pay | Nothing | Every later message, dated after the pay-by |
| Threats | A maybe in the first email | Only in the final notice, only the step he chose |
| Legal questions | Comments on state law | Applies his clause, says to check it with his lawyer, never says what a law allows |
| Next time | Start again | One question, then the next stage |

Figures from the skill's own test run of this founder. Illustrative business; plain arithmetic on his figures, not legal or tax advice.
