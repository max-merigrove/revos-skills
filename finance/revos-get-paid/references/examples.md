# Worked examples

Two complete runs, from illustrative businesses, taken from the skill's own test runs. Figures and dates are exactly what the scripts produced. Use them to see the shape of a finished plan; never copy their figures into a founder's output.

Contents: 1 A US consultant with a contract late fee and a payment plan request. 2 A UK design studio with no late fee clause. 3 A part dispute and a client to keep (US).

## 1. Ridgeline Advisory (US, USD, month-first dates)

**What the founder gave:** three clients behind. Copperline Health, $8,500 (RA-2041, invoiced 8/15, net 30), his biggest client, asked to split it; he does not want to charge them interest. Bluefin Logistics, always late: RA-2033 $4,200 (7/20) and RA-2039 $3,150 (8/1, $1,000 paid 9/10), firm email sent 9/28, monthly retainer still running, and he will pause it. Hartwell & Sons dispute RA-2050, $1,900. His clause: "Invoices unpaid 30 days after the invoice date accrue interest at 1.5% per month." He asked: "Can I actually charge that?"

**What the skill said first:** it answered his question before anything else: "It's your own term, so the plan can apply it: on Bluefin that is $186.37 so far, worked below. Whether it holds depends on the client having accepted those terms before the work and on how the clause is worded; your lawyer can confirm in one call. I'd leave it off Copperline, since you want to keep them." Then the provisional chase order table, Copperline's plan offer as a copy block, and two questions (is the interest waived for Copperline, and what name do you sign with), each saying what it changes.

**Headline:** You are owed $14,850.00 on 3 late invoices from 2 clients. Your own contract terms add $186.37 so far on Bluefin, and $3.13 a day while it stays unpaid. Copperline is left out, as he asked. $1,900.00 on the disputed invoice waits until it is settled.

| Client | How much they matter | Owed | Days late | Next message | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Copperline Health | keep at all costs | $8,500.00 | 36 | call and payment plan offer | today |
| Bluefin Logistics | always pays late | $6,350.00 | 62 | late fee notice and payment plan offer | today |

**The working (RA-2039):** due August 31; interest from September 1 at 1.5% a month, worked daily as 18% a year over 365 days: $3,150.00 for 10 days, then $2,150.00 for 40 days after the part payment = $57.95. Plain arithmetic on his own terms; check with his lawyer that the clause applies this way.

**Send now, Copperline (they asked to split it, so the plan goes first and no interest is named):**

> Subject: Invoice RA-2041: $8,500.00, 36 days overdue
>
> Hi Priya,
>
> Thanks for asking about splitting invoice RA-2041. It's $8,500.00, due on September 14, 2026, and I'm happy to make this easy.
>
> Either pay $8,500.00 by October 27, 2026, or split it into 3 installments:
> Installment 1: $2,833.33 by October 27, 2026
> Installment 2: $2,833.33 by November 27, 2026
> Installment 3: $2,833.34 by December 28, 2026
>
> Happy to jump on a quick call this week to agree which works for your team.
>
> Payment details are on the invoice. My bank details haven't changed; if you ever get a message saying they have, please call me before paying.
>
> Best,
> Jordan

**Send now, Bluefin:**

> Subject: Invoices RA-2033 and RA-2039: late fee now added
>
> Hello,
>
> Invoices RA-2033 and RA-2039 come to $6,350.00 (RA-2033, $4,200.00, due August 19, 2026; RA-2039, $2,150.00, due August 31, 2026, after your $1,000.00 on September 10). Both are still unpaid, and I last wrote on September 28, 2026.
>
> Under the late payment terms in our agreement I am now adding interest:
>
> The invoices: $6,350.00
> Interest to October 20, 2026: $186.37
> Total due: $6,536.37
>
> If $6,536.37 is paid by October 27, 2026, I'll add nothing further; after that, interest adds $3.13 a day. An updated invoice is attached.
>
> If one payment is hard right now, I can split it into 3 installments and add nothing further while they are paid on time:
> Installment 1: $2,178.79 by October 27, 2026
> Installment 2: $2,178.79 by November 27, 2026
> Installment 3: $2,178.79 by December 28, 2026
>
> Payment details are on the invoice. My bank details haven't changed; if you ever get a message saying they have, please call me before paying.
>
> Best,
> Jordan

**Ladder, Bluefin:** reminder skipped (always late); firm reminder sent September 28; late fee notice and plan today; final notice October 28, after the October 27 pay-by; stop work from November 4 is his chosen next step; a collections service or lawyer is an option to take advice on.

**Terms for future work (drafts):** his clause kept word for word; 50% deposit, on $4,200.00 that is $2,100.00 up front and $2,100.00 on delivery; 40/30/30 milestones; card or account on file for the retainer; "Enter prices before any sales tax (VAT, GST or similar); ask your accountant whether you need to charge it."

## 2. Fern Studio (UK, GBP, day-first dates)

**What the founder gave:** Harbour Dental, £2,400 (FS-104, invoiced 3 August, no terms). Kettle & Co, £850 (FS-110, 14 days from 1 September), a client she wants to keep. Greyline Logistics, FS-095 £12,500 and FS-101 £1,200, net 30, late every time, chased twice (last 1 October), retainer running; she will pause it. No late fee clause. An email to Sam for her voice.

**Headline:** You are owed £16,950.00 on 4 late invoices from 3 clients. Chase Greyline Logistics first: £13,700.00, 97 days late. Harbour's due date is a 30 day working assumption, flagged.

| Client | How much they matter | Owed | Days late | Next message | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Greyline Logistics | always pays late | £13,700.00 | 97 | call and payment plan offer | today |
| Harbour Dental Ltd | normal | £2,400.00 | 48 | firm reminder | today |
| Kettle & Co | keep at all costs | £850.00 | 35 | firm reminder | today |

**Send now, Harbour (in her voice: "Hi Sam", "shout", "sort it", "Cheers, Ana"):**

> Subject: Invoice FS-104: £2,400.00, 48 days overdue
>
> Hi Sam,
>
> Invoice FS-104 for £2,400.00 was due on 2 September 2026, so it's now 48 days overdue.
>
> Could you pay £2,400.00 by 27 October 2026? If something's holding it up, like a PO number or a question on the invoice, shout this week and I'll sort it.
>
> Payment details are on the invoice. My bank details haven't changed; if you ever get a message saying they have, please call me before paying.
>
> Cheers,
> Ana

**Send now, Greyline (no fee clause, so no fee words; the plan instead):**

> Subject: Invoices FS-095 and FS-101: £13,700.00, 97 days overdue
>
> Hello,
>
> Invoices FS-095 and FS-101 come to £13,700.00 (FS-095, £12,500.00, due 15 July 2026; FS-101, £1,200.00, due 19 August 2026). Both are still unpaid, and I last wrote about them on 1 October 2026.
>
> Please pay £13,700.00 by 27 October 2026. If one payment is hard right now, I can split it into 3 instalments:
>
> Instalment 1: £4,566.67 by 27 October 2026
> Instalment 2: £4,566.67 by 27 November 2026
> Instalment 3: £4,566.66 by 28 December 2026
>
> Reply to say which works, or call me this week.
>
> Payment details are on the invoice. My bank details haven't changed; if you ever get a message saying they have, please call me before paying.
>
> Ana

**Final notice ready for Greyline on 28 October (it names both earlier messages, and carries the bank details line again):**

> Subject: Final notice: invoices FS-095 and FS-101, £13,700.00 due by 4 November 2026
>
> Hello,
>
> This is my final notice about invoices FS-095 and FS-101, £13,700.00 in total. The first was due on 15 July 2026 and is now 105 days overdue. I wrote about them on 1 October and 20 October 2026.
>
> If £13,700.00 is not paid by 4 November 2026, I will pause any further work for you until the account is settled.
>
> Payment details are on the invoice. My bank details haven't changed; if you ever get a message saying they have, please call me before paying.
>
> Ana

**What the second run looked like (3 November):** "Using your saved clients and terms from 20 October." One question, about Greyline's £5,000.00 part payment after the last message. Her learning "plans in 2 instalments" turned Kettle's offer into £425.00 by 10 November and £425.00 by 10 December.

## 3. Reel Tide Productions (US, USD): a part dispute and a client to keep

**What the founder gave:** a QuickBooks aged receivables summary totalling $29,500.00. Nova Fitness Studio will not pay "that part" of invoice 1058 ($2,250.00 of $6,750.00). Brightwater Hotels Group, the biggest client, is 62 days late on $18,400.00 with a shoot booked for November; the founder will ask a lawyer.

**Headline:** You are owed $27,250.00 on 4 late invoices from 4 clients. Chase Brightwater Hotels Group first: $18,400.00, 62 days late. $2,250.00 of invoice 1058 is disputed and waits until settled, so $29,500.00 is open in all.

**Send now, Nova (the undisputed part is chased; the disputed part gets a call):**

> Subject: Invoice 1058: $4,500.00, 39 days overdue
>
> Hey,
>
> Invoice 1058 for $6,750.00 has $4,500.00 that is not in question. It was due on September 11, 2026 and is now 39 days overdue.
>
> On invoice 1058, I'd like to settle the $2,250.00 you raised separately: tell me a time this week and I'll call. It is not part of the amount above.
>
> Please pay $4,500.00 by October 27, 2026. If something is holding it up, like a PO number or a question on the invoice, let me know this week and I'll sort it.

**Brightwater's ladder (keep at all costs):** firm reminder October 20; call and plan November 3; final notice November 17, naming the earlier dates. Flagged: "you want to keep them and work is still going on, yet the final notice says you will ask a lawyer to recover it. Confirm that is what you want before it goes."
