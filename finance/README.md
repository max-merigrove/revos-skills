# Finance

RevOS skills for finance. One folder per skill, named `revos-<skill>`, each with a `SKILL.md`.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [revos-cash-forecast](revos-cash-forecast/) | Builds a 13-week cash forecast from a business bank export and names the first week cash drops below a safety line, in a normal and a bad quarter. |
| [revos-get-paid](revos-get-paid/) | Builds a plan to get overdue invoices paid: who to chase first, today's message in the founder's voice, dated follow-ups and a payment plan offer. |
| [revos-price-floor-calculator](revos-price-floor-calculator/) | Works out the lowest price each service offer can charge with the founder's own hours paid, and gives a raise, hold or restructure verdict. |
