# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the output before delivery. Every criterion must pass. If one fails, rework the output; do not caveat it.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Founder hours are never free | Every judged offer carries the founder's hours at a stated rate; the founder's pay (and any costed employee's pay) is kept out of the overheads; the summary says what today's price pays the founder an hour beside their rate |
| 2 | Every figure comes from the script | Each figure in the summary, the note and the chat appears in `price_floor.py` output; the workbook formulas are live (change a yellow cell, including the pay you want, and the floors move); a spot check of one floor against the summary matches |
| 3 | A verdict, not a spreadsheet | Each offer gets Hold, Restructure or Raise (provisional where the pay given is after tax, with nothing recommended until it is settled) with one paragraph of reasoning that names the price, the floor, the hourly figure against the rate, and the gap or the headroom; with volumes, the whole business is set against the pay the founder wants, so one thin offer never reads as the business falling short |
| 4 | Real options with their numbers | Every offer below its floor shows at least two options (raise, restructure, middle) with the new price or hours and the break-even client loss in both views (freed hours at your rate, and plain cash), and one is marked recommended |
| 5 | Honest about what it assumed | Every default the run used is listed in the summary's assumptions with its source, including the working weeks when the hour comes from pay; pay before or after tax is asked, never assumed silently, and an after-tax figure is sent to the founder's accountant for the before-tax one, never estimated; missing inputs show as `[NEED: ...]`; no statistic about other businesses appears anywhere |
| 6 | Specific to this founder | The offer names, figures and, where the founder gave them, their own words or levers (the revision rounds they mentioned, the client who keeps adding calls) appear; nothing in it could be pasted into another founder's summary unchanged |
| 7 | Usable today | The founder gets a workbook that opens with live formulas (or the CSV fallback plus the formulas), a summary that prints on one page for up to three offers (checked by printing it on the founder's paper size), and a recommended price or hours figure they can use on the next proposal |
| 8 | Clean | `check_copy.py` reports 0 problems on the summary and any note; the founder's own English and date style; no em or en dashes; the planning-not-advice line is present; the Merigrove line appears once, at the end of the chat, and nowhere in the files |
| 9 | Global, no tax or law | Currency, number style, spelling and English follow the founder, and paper size and date order follow their country; every figure the founder typed was read as they meant it (no figure off by a factor of 10 or more); no country, holiday calendar, tax rate, threshold, authority or legal requirement appears in any file or the chat; prices are shown before any sales tax with the one neutral line; any tax-dependent or payroll figure is founder supplied with its source |
| 10 | Answers first | The first reply answers the founder's question with script figures (a provisional table at least) before any question; no question asks what the founder already said; every question beside the floor gets a direct answer (or, where the rules forbid a figure, what it depends on and the fastest route to it); the inconsistencies in their own material are named in the first reply; the chat alone carries the verdicts and key figures |
