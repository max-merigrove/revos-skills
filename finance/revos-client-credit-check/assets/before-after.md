# Before and after: plain Claude and Client Credit Check

Landing page material. Two illustrative founders, both fictional: a freelance web developer in Leipzig weighing a 14,000 euro rebuild for a young Hamburg company that wants 60 days with everything at the end; and a brand consultant in Bengaluru weighing a 4.5 lakh project for a Pune company with an overdue filing, who invoices on the 5th of the month.

## Plain Claude (no skill)

Leipzig, no tools: a sound checklist (the register, insolvency notices, a tax-id check, a bureau report, a video call) and sensible terms (30 to 50% up front, milestones, 14 days). Then: "I can also ... run the register checks myself if you give me the exact company name", though the name was in the question. It checked nothing, gave no verdict word, and quoted two sections of German law on default and late fees.

Bengaluru, no tools, with the register text pasted: a strong answer on terms (a four-tranche table that stays near the 1 lakh limit) and good questions to ask the client ("why are the filings late?"). It also worked out the GST the founder would owe, cited two Indian statutes, guessed a second filing year was "probably also overdue", suggested searching the directors, and said "I am assuming 'invoice on the 5th' means monthly invoicing" rather than working it into the dates.

Neither reply leaves a record, says what could not be checked, or gives wording the founder can send.

## With Client Credit Check

> **Nordlicht Studios GmbH: CAUTION**, decided by two minor signals (60 days as a new client; 9 days to reply on the contract). Registered 12.09.2025, 13 months ago: above the 12-month line, not counted. Could not check: insolvency notices, judgments, reviews (no web tools).
>
> As offered you'd carry **14.000 €** for about 19 weeks. Rung 2 would carry 3.500 €, over your 3.000 € limit, so rung 3 carries **1.400 €**: 50% (7.000 €) on signing, then 3.500 €, 2.100 € and 1.400 € at stages, 7 days each.
>
> "For projects of this size I work in stages: 50% (7.000 €) when we sign, which books your start date ..."

> **Sunrise Agro Foods Pvt Ltd: CAUTION.** Because you invoice on the 5th, a stage that ends on the 30th waits five days for its invoice: rung 2 would still carry ₹1,92,857, so rung 3 carries ₹45,000, invoiced 05/12/2026, 05/01/2027 and 05/02/2027.

Plus a one-page Word brief (and PDF) with every finding's source and date, the unchecked list, the terms side by side, three pieces of wording, what would change the verdict, and a memory file that logs the client and, later, whether they paid.

## The difference in five seconds

| | Plain Claude | Client Credit Check |
|---|---|---|
| Looks the company up | Offers to, or says it could not | Searches the register when web tools exist; otherwise reads what you paste from a ten-minute lookup card |
| Verdict | None, or a view on terms only | Go, Caution or Stop from a fixed rule, with the deciding reason |
| What could not be checked | Not said | Listed with reasons; unchecked never reads as clean |
| Unpaid work you'd carry | "You'd be funding the whole project" | 14.000 € for about 19 weeks as offered; 1.400 € recommended, by script |
| Your invoicing habits | Assumed | Worked into the dates and the arithmetic |
| Law and tax | Cites statutes, works out GST | Neither; one line to check the rules where you and the client are |
| Something to send | Offers to draft later | Proposal paragraph and replies to what the client asked for, in your stage names, never mentioning the check |
| A contract that names the wrong legal form | "Have the contract corrected" | The same, as a fix-before-signing line in the brief and a one-line request to the client; Stop is kept for a different business |
| Next time | Starts again | Remembers your limit, terms, voice and every client checked |

Where plain Claude held its own: the shape of the terms was as good, and in Bengaluru it named country-specific places to look that the skill leaves to the founder and the lookup card. The skill's edge is the evidence trail, the rule, the arithmetic, the gaps and the wording, not the checklist.
