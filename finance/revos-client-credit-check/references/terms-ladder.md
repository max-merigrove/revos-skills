# The terms ladder

The lightest rung that keeps the peak unpaid work within the founder's limit, never below the rung the verdict allows. Every percentage and day count here is a working default.

## Projects

| Rung | Terms | Friction for the client | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | The founder's usual terms (default 30% up front, balance on delivery, 14 days) | None new | Go, within the limit |
| 1 | 30% deposit, balance on delivery, 14 days | Light | Go, limit slightly exceeded |
| 2 | 50% deposit, 25% at the halfway point, 25% on delivery, 14 days | Visible | Caution; sole traders above the limit |
| 3 | 50% deposit, 25% at one third, 15% at two thirds, 10% on handover, 7 days; final files and access pass over once the last payment is in | Firm | Caution with a low limit, or nothing lighter fits |
| 4 | A short first phase (about a quarter of the work) paid in full before it starts, then reassess; or decline | Strong | Stop |

## Ongoing work (retainers, monthly billing)

| Rung | Terms |
|---|---|
| 0 | The founder's usual billing (default monthly in arrears, 14 days) |
| 1 | Monthly in advance, the founder's usual days |
| 2 | Monthly in advance, 14 days, work pauses on an overdue invoice |
| 3 | Monthly in advance, 7 days, card or direct debit on file where the founder takes them, pause on overdue |
| 4 | Each month paid before it starts |

The unpaid-work peak for ongoing work is worked over the engagement (12 months when no end is given), so it is the peak of a typical cycle.

## When the client pushes back

- **"Can we do 60 days and pay at the end?"** Trade something visible. If 30 days on each stage invoice still keeps the peak within the limit (the script tests it), offer that in return for the deposit on signing. If not, offer what costs nothing: a start date fixed the day the first payment lands, and smaller invoices by stage.
- **"We don't pay deposits."** If a smaller deposit (20 points lower, the difference moved into the first stage) still fits the limit, offer it. If not, hold: the deposit is what books the start date.
- **"Bill us at the end of the month."** For ongoing work, keep the days to pay the same and bill in advance; the client's timing barely moves.
- **A client who refuses every rung** on a return run is a Stop.
- **Never** in wording: interest, late fees, legal steps, or any mention of the check.
