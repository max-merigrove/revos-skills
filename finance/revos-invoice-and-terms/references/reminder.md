# One reminder for an overdue invoice

Add the invoice to `overdue` in the inputs (number, client, contact, amount as typed, currency, the original due date, and `pay_by` if the founder gave one). The script works out the days past due and, if no pay-by date was given, proposes one a week out on a working day, said out loud as a default.

## The message

Calm, specific, one ask. In the founder's English and voice (their greeting and sign-off if known).

> Subject: Invoice 118, AUD 4,400.00, due 12 October 2026
>
> Hi Sam,
>
> Invoice 118 for AUD 4,400.00 was due on 12 October 2026, 21 days ago. Could you pay it by Monday 9 November 2026? If anything on it needs fixing, or it is waiting on something at your end, tell me and I'll sort it.
>
> The payment details and reference are on the invoice.
>
> Thanks,
> Tom

Must carry: the invoice number, the amount with its currency code, the original due date, the days past due from the script, one ask with a date, and how to pay. Never: a fee, interest, a threat, "final", a suspension of services, taking anything offline, "legal", "kindly", "friendly reminder", "per my last email", "ASAP".

## The line after it

"One reminder is a prompt; recovering a payment that has gone quiet needs a plan, with who to contact, when, and what you will and will not do. Plan that before you write a second message."

The reminder is a copy block in chat and the invoice is marked overdue in the register. No chaser sequence is written.
