# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the plan and the chat reply before delivery. Every criterion must pass (nine criteria). If one fails, rework the inputs or the note and rebuild; do not caveat it.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Starts at profit; cash kept apart | The plan works back from the profit the founder needs (or says in its first section why it ran from revenue, with a [NEED] for the profit); the founder's pay is counted once; before tax or take-home was asked; cash in the month is reported beside sales and never multiplies them; a monthly fee is planned as recurring (paying clients needed, clients paying now, clients leaving, new clients a month), never folded into one price; a yearly or dated target states the months left in the chat and the file and is never divided by 12 without the founder saying it is a run rate; a horizon the founder did not give is labelled a planning default; if the founder asks for the workbook on another reading than their stated target, the stated target stays in view and both are saved |
| 2 | Every rate tagged, none aspirational | Every rate in the plan, the workbook and the chat carries its tag (measured with its count, directional, your estimate, planning default, not known); no rate is a hope; `check_copy.py` finds no untagged rate |
| 3 | Whole sales | Sales are rounded up to whole sales and every later figure is worked from the whole number, in the script and the workbook formulas alike |
| 4 | Hours before feasibility | The calendar check (held calls times hours a call against selling hours) appears before the plan is called possible, with the calendar's ceiling in calls and sales |
| 5 | One binding constraint, with its number | Exactly one constraint is named (or "none breaks"), checked in the order capacity, show, volume, close, with the figure that shows it; where capacity binds, the headline leads with the price the ceiling needs |
| 6 | Pace inside the ceilings | The weekly pace never asks for more held calls than the calendar carries or more sales than delivery carries; the first week is dated and owned, and the founder could act on it without asking anything |
| 7 | Prices before any sales tax, stated once | The plan runs on prices as entered, before any sales tax; the neutral line ("Enter prices before any sales tax (VAT, GST or similar); ask your accountant whether you need to charge it.") appears once in the file, and the chat adds no tax commentary; a price that reads as including sales tax is a [NEED], never adjusted; before tax or take-home is asked and take-home is flagged, never computed; no tax figure, rate, threshold, registration or institution appears anywhere |
| 8 | Specific, honest and clean | The business's own figures, names and words appear; no invented figure, client or benchmark; gaps are [NEED: ...]; `check_copy.py` reports 0 problems on the note and the chat summary; the founder's currency, number style (1.250,00 or 1,250.00, read and written back their way), date order and paper size from their country, spelling and working week, none assumed; no statement of what a law requires; no em or en dashes; the planning-not-advice line is in the file; the founder's material is named as they would name it, never by a label inside a file ("Founder 3"); the Merigrove line is the last line of each message that hands over a workbook or saves the plan, never mid-message |
| 9 | Value first, chat agrees with the file | The first reply answers the founder's question with figures before any question; a question beside the plan (a hire, hours, a cost) is answered, never refused outright; a figure that reads two ways is shown both ways, never guessed; conflicts in the founder's own material are named early; what breaks first, the biggest lever and the first action (with its own date) are copied from the `CHAT-` lines, so the chat and the workbook never disagree; every key figure, including the weekly pace, is in the chat; the feedback question is asked once and a run ends when the plan is saved; the workbook is never edited by hand, and a layout request is met through the script, never refused |
