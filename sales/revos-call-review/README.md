# Call Review

Find the minute your last sales call was lost. Paste a transcript; get a scored review, two coaching points with the words to say, and one drill for your next call. Built by Merigrove for founders who take their own sales calls, or manage someone who does.

## Install

**Claude (web or desktop app)**
1. Download `revos-call-review.zip` (do not unzip it).
2. In Claude, open Customize, then Skills, then Upload a skill, and choose the zip. Code execution needs to be on (Settings, Capabilities).
3. Start a new chat and paste the first prompt below.

**Cowork:** Customize, Skills, Install, choose the zip.
**Claude Code:** unzip into `~/.claude/skills/`.

## Your first prompt

> Use revos-call-review to review this sales call. They said they'd think about it and went quiet. (attach or paste the transcript from Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Fathom or Otter)

No recording? Say "recall" and it walks you through what to describe.

## What you get

One Word document, `call-review-<initials>-<date>.docx`:

- **A score out of 100** across five weighted parts of the call, with any cap explained. It is not rounded up to be kind.
- **The two things to change,** each with the moment, the exact quote, what it caused, and the line to say next time.
- **One drill** to rehearse out loud before your next call.
- **Where the deal was lost:** each failure traced from where it showed up to where it was created ("appeared at 40:18, created at 16:32").
- **Every objection** with the belief underneath it and what was missing in how it was handled.
- **The next step graded,** with the closing exchange quoted.
- **Stated reason and real reason** kept apart, so "timing" after a call that never made the cost of waiting real is named for what it was.
- **Red lines:** the price said before the cost, deadlines, promises you cannot back.

Every finding quotes the transcript word for word at its timestamp, and a script checks each quote before you see it.

It works wherever you sell. The review uses your currency, your date order and your spelling, read from what you write, and quotes your price exactly as it was said on the call ("$18,500", "ten grand", "one and a half lakh plus GST"), never converted and with no tax worked out.

## It remembers you

After each review it offers to save your offer, who sells, your call structure, your currency and date style, and one line of scores. It never saves the transcript, a quote or a prospect's name. After three reviews of the same seller it can tell a bad day from a pattern, and when the same part of the call is weak again, that becomes the drill.

## Safety

Read SKILL.md before installing; it is plain text. This skill makes no network calls and sends nothing anywhere. Prospects appear by initials only. Check the consent rules for recording calls where you and the prospect are. It is coaching, not legal or tax advice.
