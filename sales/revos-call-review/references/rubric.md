# Rubric: what an exceptional call review looks like

Score the review before the founder sees it. Every criterion must pass. If one fails, fix the verdicts and render again; do not caveat a weak review.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every finding is evidenced | Each dimension, stage, objection, creation point and coaching point cites a position and quotes the transcript word for word (`check_review.py` confirms every one), or, in a recall review, carries "(recall, unverified)" and no time or verbatim quote |
| 2 | Two coaching points that change the next call | Exactly two; each has the moment, "Because ... the prospect did ...", one tangible fix and the line to say in quotation marks; they are the two moments that cost the deal most, not the two lowest scores; no framework named at the seller |
| 3 | Every failure traced | Each confirmed failure and a failed next step has cause, effect and "appeared at X, created at Y", with Y before X |
| 4 | The score is honest | Every number comes from `score_call.py`; caps applied where a failure was confirmed; no dimension scored on warmth; Close Attempt at 4 or under when the prospect held the next step; nothing rounded up |
| 5 | The prospect is never blamed | No blame, no judgement of the prospect's character; a prospect who should not have been on the call is named as a booking or qualifying fix; prospects by initials only; people the prospect named appear by name only inside quotes, Say lines and the message to send |
| 6 | The drill can be done tonight | One behaviour, a number of repetitions and a deadline before the next call, done out loud with nothing else to read or watch; aimed at the repeated dimension when the pattern check fires |
| 7 | Stated and real reasons kept apart | On a lost deal, the stated reason and the real reason (one of the nine, with its position) are separate lines, and "timing" is never the real reason when the cost of waiting was never made real; on an open deal, the risk is named |
| 8 | Specific and clean | Every Say instead line uses details from this call, so it could not be pasted into another founder's review; `check_review.py` reports 0 problems; written in the founder's English, currency and date style, with prices exactly as said; no law stated, no tax worked out, no country assumed, and recording covered by the one generic consent line; no Merigrove line in the file |

A review that passes 1 to 8 but would not change what the seller says on their next call has failed criterion 2. Fresh-eyes test: read only the two coaching points and the drill. If the seller did nothing else, would the next call go differently?
