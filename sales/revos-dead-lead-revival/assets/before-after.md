# Before and after

Same founder, same paste: a US brand consultant in Austin, Texas, with 63 lines of messy notes and three packages ($1,500 audit, $4,800 sprint, $2,000 a month retainer). "Before" is plain Claude, no skill, no tools, run headless on November 3, 2026. "After" is this skill, run on the same paste the same day.

| | Plain Claude | Dead Lead Revival |
|---|---|---|

| Everyone accounted for | Names people in seven waves and a don't-contact table, with no count of who went where. | 63 rows in: 53 on Contacts, 10 on Held back, each with a reason and what would release them. |

What plain Claude did well: it merged the two duplicates (Tom Webb and Hannah B), held back James, Josh, Martin and Peter for the right reasons, spotted the 2027 date on Fiona, asked whether Dan's "January" had a year, said not to send Nathan a "who are you?" email, and suggested texting Amanda, who has only a number. It also noticed that Mike T works at Tomlin & Co, the agency Olivia chose, and kept him off the list. The skill holds the same people back (Peter, Martin and Josh after Claude corrects the script's reading from the notes, each shown to the founder as a CORRECTED line), holds Amanda and Erin until Jo allows a text or a DM, and places Mike T with the people met at events; spotting his link to Tomlin & Co rests on Claude reading the list, not on the scripts.
