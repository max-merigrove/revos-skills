# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the built lead magnet and its launch pack before delivery. Every criterion must pass. If one fails, fix the spec and rebuild; never caveat it.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One problem in the buyer's words | The topic names one problem a buyer would phrase the same way, with the line it came from quoted in the pack (or marked inferred and said so in the chat). Questions or items come from the founder's own material, not generic sector content |
| 2 | Never the offer's outcome | The magnet gives clarity about the problem, not the result the offer sells, and leaves exactly one natural next question, named in the pack and on the page |
| 3 | Fit is tested | At least two fit signals (fit questions and the "not for" line). A reader who is not a fit is told so plainly and kindly, sees the full result, and is not shown the booking button. A top-tier reader sees an honest top-tier line, and every "what is working" line matches the answer given |
| 4 | The scoring is proved | `simulate` passes: every possible total lands in exactly one tier, no tier under 10 or over 60 per cent of answer sets, a middle pattern lands in a middle tier; the browser result matches the script; What is working always appears; no tier name is a verdict |
| 5 | No unsourced claim | Claim scan zero across the page, the printable copy, the pack and the emails. Every figure is the founder's with its source; anything else is cut or held as `[NEED]` off the page |
| 6 | Consumable in minutes | Scorecard under three minutes by estimate; checklist on two pages or fewer; guide on one; nothing padded |
| 7 | A page that works and looks set | Audit passes: no network call, form, storage or placeholder; keyboard and phone usable at 375 pixels; contrast 4.5 to 1 or better; no boxes or rules; nothing invented in the brand. A stranger would publish it as it stands |
| 8 | The founder's own, end to end | Their English, currency, number style, date order, sign-off and never-words; the page, the opt-in copy and email one read as one voice; the chat carries the topic, the format, the tier shares and the flags in five lines |
