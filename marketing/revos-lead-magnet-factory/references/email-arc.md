# The five emails

The follow-up is part of the lead magnet. A magnet with no follow-up is a download nobody remembers. Five emails, written before anything goes live, in the founder's voice: from one sample of their own writing when they give one, otherwise plain and direct.

## The rules block comes first

Write these into the spec (`launch.rules`) before any email:

- **What starts it:** the reader confirms their address on the opt-in form.
- **What stops it:** a booked call (take them out the same day); a reply that asks about the offer (answer personally, take them out).
- **Who is removed before it is switched on:** current clients, anyone already in a conversation with the founder, test addresses.
- **Timing:** days 0, 1, 3, 5 and 7 unless the founder says otherwise. A tool that cannot tag readers by tier is fine: email two carries every tier.

## The arc

| Email | Day | Its one job | The ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Deliver the magnet (`{{magnet_link}}`, the only place it appears) and say how to read the result | Soft: open it, start with what is working |
| 2 | 1 | The weak spot by result: one short paragraph per tier, each naming the tier exactly as the page does. For a checklist, the step readers most often get wrong, from the founder's own words | Reply with your score (or the step you are stuck on) |
| 3 | 3 | A worked example, labelled "an illustration, not a client story", unless the founder supplied a client story word for word with permission | Soft: notice whether it sounds familiar |
| 4 | 5 | The door: the question the magnet cannot answer, in the reader's terms | Soft: a question to think about, or reply |
| 5 | 7 | The one invitation: who the offer is for and who it is not for, plainly, then one booking link (`{{booking_link}}`, the only place it appears) | Hard, once |

## Per email

- Two subject lines under 50 characters, both true to the body (A/B).
- Preview text that says the next thing, never a repeat of the subject.
- A body of 120 to 220 words on one idea, `{{first_name}}` in the greeting, at most one link.
- The founder's sign-off, exactly as they write it.
- A P.S. that points to the next email or adds one useful line (email one may drop it if the founder asks).
- No figures, percentages or "most people" unless the founder's own with a source in `facts`. No false urgency, no "just checking in", no promise of the outcome the offer sells.
- The email tool adds the unsubscribe link and the sender's details; the pack reminds the founder to check both.

## Return runs

A second magnet's emails differ from the first: no subject line repeats, and no body shares a run of twelve or more words with the earlier pack (`emails.py check --previous <old spec>`, or `previous_subjects` from memory).
