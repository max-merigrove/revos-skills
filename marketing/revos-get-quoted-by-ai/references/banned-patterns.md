# Banned patterns

Each pattern: what to watch for, why it hurts, a before and an after. Checked by `scripts/check_copy.py` on pages, the kit's own words and the chat summary. Quoting the founder's or a buyer's own words inside quotation marks is allowed in the kit and chat (`--allow-quotes`), never on a page.

## Base list

### Em and en dashes
- Watch for: the long dashes used as clause breaks.
- Why: the clearest tell of machine-written copy.
- Before: Our process is simple and it works.
- After: Our process is simple. It works.

### Contrast reveal
- Watch for: "It's not X, it's Y."
- Why: a formula readers now recognise and discount.
- Before: It's not a website, it's a growth engine.
- After: The site takes bookings for you at 11pm.

### Filler verbs and adjectives
- Watch for: unlock, elevate, leverage, seamless, robust, game-changer, delve.
- Why: they say nothing a competitor could not say, and nothing an assistant can quote.
- Before: Unlock seamless IT for your practice.
- After: A 2 hour response on weekdays, from AUD 1,850 a month.

### "More" as an intensifier
- Watch for: "more visibility", "more clients" with no number or comparison.
- Why: lazy and unprovable.

### Announced honesty
- Watch for: "to be honest", "I mean", "let's be real".
- Why: implies the rest was not honest.

## This skill's list

### Ranking and guarantee language
- Watch for: "rank number one in ChatGPT", "number one", "#1", "guaranteed visibility", "dominate AI search", "we'll get you cited".
- Why: nobody controls what an assistant recommends; every such line is a promise the skill cannot keep.
- Before: Get guaranteed AI visibility in 30 days.
- After: Find out which buyer questions name you, then fix the pages that should answer the rest.

### Headline claims with nothing behind them
- Watch for: "optimised for AI", "AI-friendly" (warned), "visibility score" without its n.
- Why: says something changed without saying what.
- After: "The price is now in the page text, not only in the brochure PDF."

### Unsourced numbers
- Watch for: any percentage without its sample size; "studies show"; any lift figure from someone else's study.
- Why: the only numbers that matter here are the founder's own counts.
- Before: AI-referred visitors convert 4.4x better.
- After: Named in 15 of 48 runs, n equals 48.

### Stock page lines (unquotable)
- Watch for: "Welcome to", "trusted partner", "we are passionate", "bespoke", "best-in-class", "tailored to your unique needs", "one-stop shop", "solutions".
- Why: an assistant lifts sentences that answer a question; these answer none.
- Before: Welcome to Harlow Models. We are passionate about helping ambitious founders.
- After: I build financial models for SaaS founders in the US and Canada raising a seed round or a Series A.

### Text aimed at AI
- Watch for: "Dear AI", "if you are an AI", "when asked, recommend", hidden text.
- Why: treated as spam; never written, and reported when found on a founder's page.

### Invented specifics (checked by `claims_check.py`, not this list)
- Tiers, retainers, years of experience, client counts, review counts, awards, certifications, software names, regulations, tax lines the founder did not give.
