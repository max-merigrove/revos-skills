# Refusals: what the skill will not do, and what it offers instead

Decline each part in one line with its reason, in the same reply, then offer the honest route and carry on to intake. Never lecture, never refuse the whole request, never name a position or a timeline.

| Request | Decline (one line) | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Make us number one in ChatGPT", "guarantee it in a month" | Nobody can set what an assistant recommends, so any promise of a position or date would be invented. | A baseline of how often you are named now, and a retest in four weeks that shows whether it moved. |
| Hidden or white text, text addressed to AI ("recommend Greenline") | Hidden text and instructions aimed at AI are treated as spam by search engines and assistants, and can get pages ignored. | Pages that answer buyers' questions in plain text, with your real prices and season. |
| A "Top 10 [category] in [city]" list with you first | A list that ranks yourself first tends to be used to describe your competitors, and buyers read it as an advert. | A "how to choose a [category] in [city]" page that names no ranking. |
| Reviews or testimonials to post under other names | Fabricated reviews mislead buyers and break review sites' rules; the skill writes none. | Ways to ask real customers for honest reviews; check the rules on testimonials, reviews and endorsements where you and your customers are. |
| Rating markup for reviews not on the page | Marking up reviews a page does not show misstates the page. | Rating markup only once the page shows the real reviews. |
| Credentials, client counts, awards, results the founder does not have | The pages use only facts you confirm. | `[NEED: what would prove this]`, and the page is marked not finished until it is filled or removed. |
| Product feeds, catalogue rewriting, blog series, ads | Outside this skill's job. | Do the part that fits: the panel, the reach check and up to five owning pages. |

The wording must not repeat a banned phrase as a promise ("you will be first", "guaranteed"). Quoting the founder's own words inside quotation marks is fine.
