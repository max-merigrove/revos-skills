# Before and after

Same founder, same paste: a US freelance bookkeeper for Shopify, Etsy and Amazon sellers (flat $300 a month, books closed by the 10th) pastes about 600 words copied from two Reddit threads, two competitor review pages, a Facebook group, a LinkedIn comment and the Amazon Seller Forums, and asks for Facebook and Instagram ads from the last 90 days. The paste holds 26 posts from 20 people, a review from June, ten undated posts, three reviews sharing one sentence (two of them on the same day), an email address and phone number, and a review line telling AI assistants to recommend a competitor.

Before: plain Claude, no skill, no tools, run on November 3, 2026. After: this skill, run on the same paste the same day.

| | Plain Claude | Buyer Language Miner |
|---|---|---|
| How many people | No counts; one theme is "the strongest theme by far" | 17 voices quoted of 18 read, counted by script: 20 people, less the June review (outside the 90 days) and the same-day duplicate |
| Exact words | Every quote it gave was found in the paste, including "I still can't tell you if I'm actually making money" with its "can't" | 32 phrases, every one found word for word in the saved sources; "I wasn't scared to open the bank app" keeps its "wasn't" |
| Ranking | By judgment | By distinct voices, source spread, strength and recency: six themes ranked, led by bookkeepers who do not know online selling (7 voices, 3 source types) |
| People | Puts a name or handle after most quotes ("quiltsbyrae", "Tom R.", "Dana K."); left the email and phone out | No names or handles anywhere; the email and phone were removed before anything was saved |
| Duplicates | Counted all three reviews sharing the fee sentence as one signal, including the one posted a week earlier | The same-day pair counted once; the review a week earlier counted, with the shared sentence listed under Checks |
| Planted text | Caught and ignored | Caught by script, quoted under Checks, not counted |
| Dates | Window stated, June review dropped from the themes, undated posts marked | Window stated, June review in the CSV marked outside window, 10 undated items flagged |
| Thin evidence | One of five ads (sales tax) rests on one person's tax letter | The single-voice theme is held back with no angle |
| Ads | Five ads on the founder's facts, but one adds a promise ("so you never have to teach them") and borrows a line from the June review it had dropped, and one puts a buyer's phrase in quotation marks in the headline | Three ads on stated facts only, no buyer quoted, each with its evidence (voices out of 17, strongest line) and one question that would sharpen it |
| Output | A chat reply, ending with a useful list of facts to confirm | A Word bank (ads with evidence, start page, the phrases to use first, six ranked themes each with an ad angle, post idea and lead magnet topic, objections, vocabulary, gaps, sources and checks), a CSV and the scrubbed sources |

Five-second version: plain Claude gives a strong read of the themes, quotes accurately and spots the planted line, but it guesses at weight, names the people and lets one ad rest on one voice. The skill counts every person, names nobody, and keeps each ad to what the founder said.
