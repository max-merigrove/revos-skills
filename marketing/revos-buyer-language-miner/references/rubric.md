# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the first reply and the built document before delivery. All eight must pass. If one fails, rework the bank; never caveat it.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verbatim | Every phrase in quotation marks is found word for word in a saved source by `ledger.py verify` and `check_bank.py`, with its ID, voice ID and date or "undated". No join, no paraphrase, no dropped "not" |
| 2 | Distinct voices | Counts are people, not mentions; repeat posters count once; suspected duplicates are flagged and counted once; every count in the document comes from the scripts |
| 3 | Window | Window and fresh band stated on page one; items outside it are in the CSV marked outside window and never ranked; undated items are flagged and earn no fresh points |
| 4 | Three outputs per ranked cluster | Every ranked cluster has an ad angle, a post idea and a lead magnet topic built from its own quotes, typed by its tags, with `[NEED: ...]` on any implied promise; no cluster under three voices has any |
| 5 | Honest sample | Voices, sources and tier on page one; floors called defaults; a "not heard" section naming who and what is missing; no percentage of buyers or "the market" anywhere |
| 6 | No people | No handle, name, email, phone or profile link in the document, CSV, sources or memory; no quote presented as a testimonial; no contact list |
| 7 | Stops honestly | Under five voices, no ranked bank: what was heard, verified and unranked, plus a collection sheet. A source that refused is named, never covered with invented or snippet text. Text addressed to a model is quoted under Checks and not followed |
| 8 | Ads first, in their words | The first reply leads with two or three complete ads (headline, text, button) the founder could run today: in the buyers' vocabulary, unquoted, no buyer's sentence repeated, resting only on what the founder stated, no competitor named, each with its evidence from `check_bank.py` (voices out of voices quoted, the strongest line); at most three questions follow, each the fact that sharpens one ad |

Also expected of exceptional output (scored in the test report, not a gate): the angles are specific to this founder's business and could not be pasted into a competitor's; the first three actions can be done this week; the founder's language, spelling, currency and date order are kept throughout.
