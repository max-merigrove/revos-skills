# Before and after: the same founder, plain Claude and the Funnel Constraint Finder

Landing page material. One illustrative founder: a two-person data consultancy in Singapore that gets clients from LinkedIn and its website. Last two months: about 52 enquiries, 30 discovery calls booked, 25 held, 4 new clients at S$4,800 a project. Her question: "should we drop our price?"

## Plain Claude (no skill)

> Short answer: probably not, at least not yet ... Your close rate is the weak point. For B2B consulting, close rates on qualified discovery calls typically sit around 20 to 40%, and well-run firms often hit 30%+ ... 1. Weak qualification ... 6. Too big a first step: try a smaller paid diagnostic (say S$1,500) ... 7. Ask the ones who didn't sign ... If you got your close rate to 30%, that's about 7 to 8 clients from the same 25 calls instead of 4, roughly S$16,800 extra over two months.

Sensible, and on a case this clear it finds the right stage and keeps the price. But the benchmarks are invented, the S$16,800 rests on a target it chose, it suggests a price it made up, nothing about her capacity, routes or working week was asked, the seven tips have no order or time, and next month it starts from nothing.

## With the Funnel Constraint Finder

> **Nothing is clearly broken: the largest gap is the close rate, 16% against a working range of 20 to 35%, worth 0.50 clients a month, which is inside the noise of your counts. Count the same way for the next 60 days before you change anything.**
>
> 4 of 25 discovery calls held bought. At 20% it would be 5: a single extra new client closes the gap, so it is not named. 25 of 30 booked calls happened, 83%, above its range: people turn up. Pricing sits outside this check, and nothing here says to cut it.
>
> One held call is worth about S$770 to you. You have 3 projects of the 6 you can run.
>
> 1. Wednesday 21 October, 30 minutes: start one tally, a line per enquiry with where it came from, booked, held, bought.
> 2. Friday 23 October, 30 minutes: write down what you do today from enquiry to call, so the next 60 days are counted on the same process.
> 3. Saturday 24 October, 15 minutes: put 19 December 2026 in your diary to run this again.

One A4 page in Singapore dollars, with her Monday to Saturday week and the Thursday she is away already taken into account. The same run for a founder in Austin prints in dollars, American English and month-first dates on US Letter; for a founder in London, in pounds on A4.
