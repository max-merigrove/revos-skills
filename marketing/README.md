# Marketing

RevOS skills for marketing. One folder per skill, named `revos-<skill>`, each with a `SKILL.md`.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [revos-vsl-writer](revos-vsl-writer/) | Writes a VSL or call-funnel video script from a fact-gathering intake, using a 14-section flow (hook, credibility, opportunity, price-first offer, objections, fit, CTA, proof, plus a skeptic pass for long versions), then QA-checks it. |
| [revos-funnel-constraint-finder](revos-funnel-constraint-finder/) | Finds the one stage of a founder's sales funnel costing the most customers, from their own rough counts, and prices the fix in clients and money a month. |
| [revos-buyer-language-miner](revos-buyer-language-miner/) | Writes two or three ready-to-run ads in the words buyers actually used, backed by a bank of every phrase checked word for word and counted by how many different people said it. |
| [revos-get-quoted-by-ai](revos-get-quoted-by-ai/) | Tests whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot name a business for its buyers' questions, then writes the pages, structured data and llms.txt they can quote. |
| [revos-lead-magnet-factory](revos-lead-magnet-factory/) | Builds a finished scorecard or checklist lead magnet from the founder's own material, with the opt-in page and five follow-up emails that move the right people towards a call. |
