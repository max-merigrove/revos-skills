# Operations

RevOS skills for operations. One folder per skill, named `revos-<skill>`, each with a `SKILL.md`.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [revos-delivery-capacity-check](revos-delivery-capacity-check/) | Works out how many clients a founder can hold per offer and the three delivery tasks to hand over first. For service businesses of one to five people; does the sums with a script and keeps a memory file between runs. |
| [revos-founder-time-audit](revos-founder-time-audit/) | Audits where a founder's week goes, ranks what to stop and hand over by the hours it gives back, costs each handover and writes the handover brief. |
| [revos-hiring-readiness](revos-hiring-readiness/) | Tests whether a small business can afford and needs its next hire: Ready, Not yet or No, the most the hire can cost, then the scorecard, job ad and first 90 days. |
| [revos-scope-guard](revos-scope-guard/) | Builds a scope kit for a service founder: a scope statement, what extras have cost, and the words to price extra work. |
