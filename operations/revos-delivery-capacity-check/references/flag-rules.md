# Flag rules: F, W or T for every delivery step

## 1. The three flags and the decision rule

| Flag | Meaning | Rule | Moves when |
|---|---|---|---|
| F | Founder | The founder's judgement is what the client pays for | Not at this stage. Later, only once the brand can carry the offer without the founder's name on the work |
| W | Founder until written | The founder does it only because nobody else knows how, and it recurs and can be taught | Once a written procedure exists and someone has run it from that write-up while the founder checks |
| T | Team or tool | Rule-based or administrative; a team member, contractor or software can do it now | Now. No write-up needed beyond what exists |

One flag per step, with one line on why. A step without a reason is not flagged.

## 2. Four test questions, in order

Ask them of each step; the first "yes" sets the flag.

1. **Would the client notice, and mind, if someone else did this?** Mind because the founder's judgement, taste or relationship is what they bought, not because the founder is faster. Yes: **F**.
2. **Does someone else (or software) already do this, or could they do it next week with what exists today?** Yes: **T**.
3. **Does it recur, and could a capable person do it from a clear write-up?** Yes: **W**.
4. **None of the above?** It is usually a judgement step disguised as a task: split it (section 3), or flag **F** and say why.

## 3. Steps that need care

- **Split mixed steps.** "Monthly report" is often 90 per cent pulling figures (W or T) and 10 per cent commentary (F). Split it into two steps with their own hours so the handover is honest.
- **Quality checks are F** while the founder reviews (`references/method.md` section 6). A check becomes T only when someone else is named as the reviewer. Set `gate: yes` on every check of the work before the client sees it. Not every "review" is one: a design review with the client, a quarterly business review call, reviewing client feedback and logging the changes, or approving timesheets are judged by question 1 like any other step (`gate: no`). When a founder flags a step W or T whose name only says "review" or "approve", the script asks rather than overriding them.
- **Check-in calls are not quality checks.** A weekly client call is F (relationship) or W (a status update someone else could run from a template); judge it by question 1.
- **Onboarding** is usually W: the same steps each time, never written down. The welcome call can stay F while the setup moves.
- **Sales calls are not delivery.** Leave them out; they belong in selling time, which comes off the available hours.
- **Shared steps** ("me and Priya"): count them as the founder's until someone else does all of it, and note it.
- **Business-wide steps** (team standups, reviewing all the team's work, one dashboard for every client) are flagged like any other step, but set on `business`, not divided across clients.
- **"I'm the only one who can do it"** is the commonest W step flagged F. Ask: is the client paying for your judgement here, or is it just that nobody else has been shown?
- **The opposite pull:** a founder keen to hire may flag judgement work T. Hold the line; flags that flatter a hire break the ceiling the first month.

## 4. Examples by kind of business

Illustrative steps and the flag they usually take. Always judge the founder's own step by section 2; these are starting points, not rules.

**Coaching or mentoring programme**
- One-to-one sessions: F (the coach's judgement is the product).
- Session prep from notes: W if it is pulling last session's actions into a template; F if it is deciding the next intervention.
- Scheduling, reminders, payment chasing: T.
- Workbook or resource updates: W.
- Community or group chat replies: W for logistics, F for coaching questions; split them.

**Marketing or social media agency retainer**
- Strategy and campaign angle: F.
- Content calendar and drafting posts: W once the voice and examples are written down.
- Scheduling posts, uploading assets: T.
- Ad account adjustments: W (rule-based once thresholds are written).
- Monthly report: figures W or T, commentary F.
- Checking drafts before the client sees them: F until someone else reviews.

**Consultancy project**
- Diagnosis and recommendations: F.
- Desk research and data gathering: W.
- Slide production from the founder's outline: W, often T with a template.
- Workshop facilitation: F at this stage.
- Final check of the deliverable: F.

**Bookkeeping or accounting practice**
- Data entry and bank feeds: T.
- Bank reconciliation: W (checklist-led).
- Chasing receipts and documents: W, often T with templated emails.
- Payroll run: W.
- Month-end review and sign-off: F while the founder signs off.
- Advice questions from clients: F.

**Design or creative studio**
- Concept and direction: F.
- Production and artwork variations: W.
- File preparation and export: T or W.
- Client feedback rounds: F for the creative call, W for logging and actioning changes.
- Final check against the brief: F.

**Trades, installation or field services**
- Survey and quote: F while the founder prices the job.
- The job itself: W where the method is standard and a skilled operative can follow it; F for specialist work.
- Ordering materials, booking, invoicing: T.
- Sign-off and snagging check: F until a named supervisor reviews.

**Training, workshops or courses**
- Delivering the session: F.
- Preparing materials from the master deck: W.
- Joining instructions, registers, certificates: T.
- Marking or feedback: W against a rubric; F for judgement calls.

## 5. How to present the flags to the founder

Show the drafted steps grouped by flag, one line each, so the founder can scan the F and T lists and read the W list closely:

> Here is how I have flagged your steps. Change any that are wrong.
>
> **Yours (F)**
> 1. Monthly strategy call, 1.5 hours, monthly (they renew for your read on their market)
>
> **Yours until written down (W)**
> 2. Monthly report, 2 hours, monthly (same template every month; only the commentary is yours)
>
> **Someone or something else can do it now (T)**
> 3. Invoicing, 30 minutes, monthly (your accounting software can send it now)
>
> For the W steps near the top, will you check the work before the client sees it once someone else does it? Roughly how long?

Add the hours-check question to this message when compute printed one.

Apply any active learning on flags from the memory file before drafting (for example "strategy calls stay F").
