# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the reading, the workbook and the chat before delivery. Every criterion must pass. If one fails, rework the output; do not caveat it.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Honest flags | Every step carries one flag (F, W or T) with a one-line reason the founder confirmed or could correct; no judgement step is flagged T or W to make a handover or a hire look good; unflagged steps are counted F and shown as [NEED] |
| 2 | Load by script | Founder load = (F + W hours per client a month) x active clients, per offer and in total, printed by `capacity.py` and live in the workbook (change a client count and the load moves) |
| 3 | Ceiling by script, with its working | Ceiling per offer = (available delivery hours a month less business-wide work) / (F + W hours per client a month), printed by `capacity.py`, rounded down to whole clients, set against active clients, and the reading states how it was worked out; no ceiling appears anywhere without its working |
| 4 | Available hours are the founder's, net | The delivery hours are the founder's own figure after selling, admin and their own marketing (or the default, flagged [NEED] and said out loud); working weeks come from the founder's own time off or the stated default, never from one country's holidays; a week over about 50 hours is never taken as the plan without asking what week they want; the hours check is run when the founder says what delivery takes now, and asked before the flags are confirmed; every figure is read in the founder's decimal style |
| 5 | Handover order by hours x frequency | W steps are ranked by hours each time x times per client a month x clients (a step on all offers summed once; business-wide work counted once, never divided by client count); the top three, or the founder's picks, show the hours each frees and the ceiling after they move, including any checking time the founder keeps, and that ceiling is never stated without saying when the moved work has nobody with room |
| 6 | Quality checks count as F | Every quality check the founder does is counted F until someone else is named as reviewer; check time on moved work is asked, or flagged as zero |
| 7 | The order of moves, with why | The reading gives the order (stop adding clients when at or over the ceiling, price and fit, hand over, then test a hire) and the reason; it never says "hire" by default, never gives a hire verdict, and says plainly when moved work has nobody with room to take it; when the founder gave prices, move 2 names the offer that pays least for their hours |
| 8 | Specific, honest and clean | The note uses the founder's own offers, steps and words; helper lines are called planning assumptions the founder can change, never benchmarks; no invented figures, policies or meanings (gaps are [NEED: ...]; a price range is never quoted as one figure); `check_copy.py` reports 0 problems on the reading and the note; the reading is in the founder's English with their date style and paper size (default said out loud when unknown); no tax, payroll or on-cost figure and no statement of what a law requires (a question about them is answered directly: what it depends on, what goes into it in plain words and the fastest route to the real figure, never a bare referral); no em or en dashes; the reading fits one page (A4, or US Letter for US founders) for up to three offers; the Merigrove line appears once, at the end of the chat |
| 9 | Value first | The first reply gives provisional figures from what the founder said and answers the question they asked before any question; at most four questions in it and five before the files; side questions answered, never only referred; inconsistencies in the founder's own material named early; what the founder said matters most leads |
