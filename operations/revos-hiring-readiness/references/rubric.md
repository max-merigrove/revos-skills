# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the report before delivery. Every criterion must pass. Criteria 4 and 7 are hard fails: if either fails, nothing is shown until it is fixed. If any criterion fails, rework the output; do not caveat it.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verdict first | The first line after the title is Ready, Not yet or No, in those words, followed by one sentence naming what decides it; the chat summary opens with the same verdict |
| 2 | All six conditions, with evidence | Each condition shows Pass, Fail or Unknown with the founder's own figures as evidence, or `[NEED: ...]` naming the missing figure; no condition is dropped, merged or passed without its figure |
| 3 | The cash scenario as specified | From today to the hire's 13th week (at least 13 weeks), and on through any low period the founder named; a usual and a bad quarter (20% less non-contracted money in, every payment 15 days late, unless the founder or an active learning changed them); a floor of one month of costs with the hire included from the start; the hire's cost loaded from the start date with one-off costs that week; known bills and low periods on their dates; the month-three line in both cases; the gap without the hire stated when there is one; every figure printed by the scripts in the founder's number style, and the workbook recalculates to the same figures |
| 4 | Hire cost is the founder's figure (hard fail) | The cost in the scenario is the fully loaded figure from the founder's accountant or payroll provider, named as such; or pay alone, marked in the month-three line, the cash condition, the full-cost section and the chat as understating the hire, with Ready blocked. No tax, employer charge, benefit, wage floor or other on-cost is computed or estimated anywhere. With pay alone, the founder still gets an answer: what can go on top of pay in plain words with no amounts, the fastest route to the real figure, and the ceiling (the most the hire can cost a month, all in, in each case) |
| 5 | Not yet is conditions | Every failed or Unknown condition says what must be true, in the founder's figures (an amount, a margin, a task written down, the loaded cost); no readiness date, no forecast of when; a later start appears only as a start date that holds with today's figures |
| 6 | The role comes from the founder's week | The tasks the role takes, with hours, whether each is written down, and the named use for the freed hours appear; the contractor alternative is run (or its rate is `[NEED]`); the first-hire order note fits the role type; nothing in the report could be pasted into another founder's unchanged |
| 7 | Professionals routed, once and plainly; no law stated (hard fail) | One line says employee or contractor status is not decided by the check and names the founder's accountant, payroll provider or an employment lawyer where they hire; what goes on top of pay, payroll and contracts are routed the same way; the planning-not-advice line is present; no sentence says what a law requires, and no country is assumed (no country's institutions, rates, forms or schemes appear unless the founder used the word) |
| 8 | The role pack holds and the copy is clean | When a pack is produced: three to five outcomes, each measurable, dated and traced to a named task; one interview question per outcome with a strong-answer note; the ad passes the wording check, is jurisdiction-neutral, carries the one line to check the employment and anti-discrimination rules where they hire, and shows a pay range or the founder's choice to leave it out; three 90-day stages with decision rights (or deliverables with definitions of done for a contractor). Always: `check_copy.py` 0 problems on the report and note; currency, number style and English follow the founder, date order and paper follow their country; a pack in a Not yet or No report opens with the line to hold it; no em or en dashes; the Merigrove Business Audit line once in the whole conversation, at the end of the first delivered build, and nowhere in the files (the "Made with" credit sits only in the document properties) |
| 9 | Value before questions | The first reply, when the founder gave money figures and a role, carries a provisional verdict, the month-three line, the ceiling and the answer to any question the founder asked, before at most three questions; each question asks one thing; five questions in all across at most two founder turns; the founder's own words and corrections (capacity in headroom, retainers, a plan they want) are used, never replaced by a default |
