# What clients assume is included, by kind of work

Use this to fill the not-included list. Pick the three to five that fit this founder's offer, then add every out-of-scope thing this client has actually asked for. These are working lists drawn from common service-business disputes, not survey results; never quote them to a founder as statistics.

## Contents

Social media and content, web design and development, brand and graphic design, copywriting, marketing and ads management, SEO, PR, consulting and coaching, bookkeeping and accounts, virtual assistance and admin, photography and video, events, training and workshops.

## Social media and content

- Paid ads, boosting posts or ad budget management
- Replying to comments and messages outside agreed hours
- Extra reels, stories or posts beyond the monthly number
- Photography or filming (as opposed to editing supplied content)
- Weekend or evening posting and live coverage of events
- Influencer outreach and partnerships
- Newsletters and email campaigns

## Web design and development

- Extra pages beyond the agreed number, including landing pages
- Writing the copy (as opposed to placing supplied copy)
- Sourcing or buying stock images and fonts
- Hosting, domains and third-party subscriptions
- Ongoing updates, plugin updates and security after launch
- Online shop set-up, booking systems and integrations
- Further rounds of design changes after sign-off

## Brand and graphic design

- Extra logo concepts or rounds beyond the agreed number
- Applying the brand to new items (signage, packaging, vehicle, merchandise)
- Print management and print costs
- Font and image licences
- A full brand guidelines document when only a logo was sold
- Social media templates

## Copywriting

- Extra pages, emails or posts beyond the agreed count
- Rewrites after a change of direction or new information
- Research interviews with customers
- SEO keyword research
- Uploading or formatting in the client's system
- Proofreading other people's writing

## Marketing and ads management

- Ad spend itself
- New creative (images, video) beyond the agreed number
- New platforms beyond those agreed
- Landing pages and website changes
- Weekly calls or reports when the plan says monthly
- CRM or email automation set-up

## SEO

- Writing new content
- Technical fixes that need a developer
- Link-building outreach
- Local listings for additional locations
- Reporting more often than agreed

## PR

- Guaranteed coverage (never promised)
- Event management
- Crisis communications
- Media training
- Award entries

## Consulting and coaching

- Calls beyond the agreed number or length
- Messages answered outside agreed hours
- Doing the work for the client (writing their emails, building their spreadsheets) when the offer is guidance
- Sessions with the client's team as well as the client
- Documents, templates or decks built for the client
- Attending the client's meetings

## Bookkeeping and accounts

- Catch-up work on months before the start date
- Extra bank accounts, cards or entities
- Transactions above the agreed monthly volume
- Payroll, sales tax returns or annual tax filings when not agreed
- Management accounts and forecasts
- Ad hoc queries answered as advice

## Virtual assistance and admin

- Hours beyond the agreed monthly block
- Work outside agreed hours or at weekends
- Personal (non-business) tasks
- New software set-up and training
- Customer service on the client's behalf

## Photography and video

- Extra edited images or longer edits beyond the agreed number
- Raw files
- Travel beyond the agreed area, parking and expenses
- Extra locations, outfits or hours on the day
- Licensing for paid advertising or resale
- Rush delivery

## Events

- Supplier costs and venue hire
- Changes after the final numbers date
- On-the-day staffing beyond the agreed team
- Post-event content and reporting

## Training and workshops

- Extra sessions or attendees beyond the agreed number
- Tailored materials for each team
- Follow-up one-to-one coaching
- Recording and editing the session
