# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the report and the workbook before delivery. Every criterion must pass. If one fails, rework the output; do not caveat it.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hours reconcile to the source | `reconcile.py` prints 0 problems: counted calendar hours equal the calendar total, rows plus "not placed" equal the week the founder gave (or the calendar plus the stated allowance), and the headline's parts add to its total. Any hours not placed are shown, never spread silently |
| 2 | One pile and one action per activity | Every row has exactly one category (only you, with instruction, nobody should) and one action (keep, delete, delegate, automate). Only-you is keep, nobody-should is delete, and a with-instruction row kept by the founder carries its reason |
| 3 | Ranked by hours, never by ease | The handover list is in order of hours a month (hours each times times a month), largest first, or in the order the founder chose with the hours-only order shown beside it; the report shows both factors and hours a week for each row. No "quick wins" or "start small" ordering of the skill's own making |
| 4 | Deletions first | "Delete first" comes before "Hand over" in the report and the workbook. Each deletion has its reason (no decision in four weeks, nobody reads it, a second check on correct work) |
| 5 | Value at the founder's rate, with its basis | The value line uses the rate the founder gave, or monthly sales over their hours, and says which. It says the hours earn that only if they go into paid or selling work, shows helper cost where the founder gave it, and never presents time as cash. No rate: `[NEED]`, not an invented one |
| 6 | Only-you challenged once | Every only-you row that reads as admin, reporting or scheduling was questioned once, and the founder's answer is in the report under "Questioned once", whether it stayed or moved |
| 7 | Recall is labelled | A recall-only audit says "Low confidence" at the top of the report with the reason, rows from memory are marked in a mixed audit, and the one-week log is offered for a proper run |
| 8 | Usable, specific and private | The top handover names a real person or tool and could start next week inside that person's stated spare hours; the proposed week is there, with a protected strategy block when Strategy is under 15%; no personal entry is named in any file or the chat; `check_copy.py` prints 0 problems; the Merigrove line appears once in the whole conversation, on the message that delivers the files, and nowhere in the files; the first reply gave a provisional answer and the calendar's clashes before any question; the chat summary carries the key figures; a handover brief for the first helper is delivered with every unknown in brackets, nothing invented |
| 9 | The founder's own locale, and in scope | Currency, number style, time zone, working week (including different hours on some days) and holidays are the founder's (from their calendar or their words); paper, date order and clock follow their country, never their spelling; every figure they typed was read as they meant it (multipliers, day ranges, decimal commas) and the headline figures passed the plausibility checks; no tax, payroll, on-cost or legal figure is computed and no law is stated; the employment-status line, if any, points to their accountant or an employment adviser without naming a country's rules |
