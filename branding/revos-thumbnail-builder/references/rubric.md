# Rubric: what exceptional looks like

Score the output before delivery. Every criterion must pass. If one fails, rework the output; do not caveat it.

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reads at real size | Every delivered thumbnail passes the hook size, contrast, squint, highlight and keep-clear checks, and on looking at the 168 px copy the words read and the face is still a face; every letter is drawn in a face that has it and shaped correctly (no empty boxes, no unjoined or reordered letters) |
| 2 | Designed, not templated | Judged as a professional thumbnail designer would: the key word dominates, the spacing is tight and deliberate, no outline or drop shadow on flat colour, no colour the founder did not give, no number split from its unit; good enough to show on a landing page |
| 3 | Honest promise | The promise and payoff lines are written, the payoff has a time, every number on the thumbnail appears in the payoff (a short form rounds down, never up), and nothing promises more than the video delivers; no tax, legal or accounting claim of the skill's own |
| 4 | Real options with reasons | At least three styles (or the saved style plus a challenger on a return run) that differ visibly in layout, type treatment and colour logic, not only in how the photo is framed; with brand colours at least one is on a light background; each has a reason line about this video, and one is recommended |
| 5 | Words that earn the space | Three to five hook options from different angles, two to five words each, none repeating the title, one highlight or one focal, no clickbait |
| 6 | The founder's own look | Their photo, uncropped through the eyes; their colours, font and logo where given (a colour given by name is settled from a screenshot or a swatch pick, never dropped; a font swapped only for missing letters, and they are told); plain black and white where not; their language, spelling and currency as they wrote them, no country assumed; nothing invented |
| 7 | Usable today | PNGs at the exact pixel sizes asked for, under the platform's file limit, named clearly, with the comparison sheet and notes beside them |
| 8 | Remembers | A first run proposes a memory file; a return run applies the saved style and active learnings without asking for them again, and records any click-through reported |
| 9 | Clean | 0 problems from `check`, `check_copy.py --hooks` and `memory_tool.py check`; no banned pattern in anything the founder reads |
